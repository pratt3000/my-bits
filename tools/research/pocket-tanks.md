# pocket-tanks — design brief

*Compiled from reference images the researcher downloaded and viewed.*

## Art direction

Pure black night sky that bleeds into a deep violet glow only in the last ~25% above the horizon (#000000 → #1A0026 → #3D005B → #5D008A), against acid-green destructible terrain — and the terrain is the whole visual identity. It is drawn as stacked topographic contour bands that follow the surface silhouette exactly: a 1–3px blazing rim highlight (#00BC00–#00D400), then a ~35px band of #008700–#00AA00, then ~50px of #006000–#008B00, then ~50px of #004600–#005100, then an infinite deep body of #003F00–#004A00, every band carrying ±8 per-column green jitter plus faint darker vertical brush streaks. The green channel is essentially pure — R and B sit at 0 in the terrain, which is why it looks radioactive. Sprites are tiny, chunky and 3/4-perspective: tanks are ~28px wide saucer-shaped hulls with a thin silver whip barrel, dwarfed by the landscape. Player names are huge blocky arcade caps in saturated flat colours (red #D30000/#E01010, cyan #07B0B0, yellow, blue, violet, green) with a hard black drop-shadow, hung in the top corners over the sky with a white-grey score numeral (#C2C2C2) beneath. The control deck below is a near-black machined slab of recessed bevelled plates — the mobile version is almost entirely #0A0A0A/#1C1C1C/#3C3C3C, while the 2001 PC version used lighter brushed metal (#303030 body, #797979–#C7C7C7 speculars) and a solid orange #E46602 FIRE button. Everything is high-contrast, unatmospheric, and deliberately cheap-looking in a confident 2001 shareware way.

- `#0A0A0A` — control deck slab background

## Typography

Two faces only. PRESS START 2P for player names, score numerals, the centred volley banner ("3rd Volley"), floating damage numbers, the WEAPON SHOP title and the FIRE label — the reference uses a chunky blocky pixel-arcade display face with hard black drop-shadow for exactly these elements, and Press Start 2P is the only approved font in that family. Use it sparingly and never below 10px: it is extremely wide, so cap player names at 7 characters and measureText-and-shrink for the scoreboard chips. NUNITO SANS (600/700) for every functional label and body string — "Move", "Weapon", "Angle", "Power", all weapon names, buttons, tooltips, the shop lists. The reference HUD labels and weapon-list names are a soft, rounded, generous-x-height humanist sans, and Nunito Sans matches their friendliness far better than Inter or Space Grotesk. Do NOT set weapon names in Press Start 2P — "Chain Reaction" and "Mountain Mover" will blow the row width apart. Bebas Neue is an acceptable alternate for FIRE only if Press Start 2P reads too small in the 200×72 button.

## Shape language

Two opposing languages held in tension. ABOVE the deck: organic, jagged, hand-torn — the terrain silhouette is a noisy 1px-per-column heightline with sharp cliffs, overhang-free vertical drops, and crater arcs bitten out of it; explosions are perfect concentric circles; shells are 3px dots with hairline polyline trails; tanks are squat rounded trapezoids (26px base, 16px top, 7px tall) on a rounded tread bar with 6 wheel dots, topped by a semicircular turret dome and a single 14px hairline barrel. BELOW: strictly rectilinear machined hardware — every control is a rounded rect (r=10) "recessed plate" with a 1px light top-left stroke and 1px black bottom-right stroke; value readouts are darker rounded insets (r=8) sunk into their parent plate. Two signature deviations from plain rects, both worth reproducing exactly: (1) the Angle plate has a HALF-DOME top — the upper two corners round all the way into a semicircular arch; (2) labels sit on small raised "tabs" that notch into and overlap the top border of their plate (the "Weapon" and "Angle" tabs). The Power plate contains a ruler-tick meter: a black inset bar with ~26 fine vertical ticks, overlaid left-to-right by a dark-red fill. Icons are 24×24 flat 2-colour vector glyphs (star, mushroom cloud, down-triangle, bolt, crosshair, mountain) seated on 56×56 silver bevel tiles.

## UI chrome

Deck slab: #0A0A0A with a 1px #2A2A2A top hairline and a subtle vertical gradient down to #050505, occupying the bottom ~38% of the portrait screen. Plate recipe (use everywhere): roundRect r=10, fill #1C1C1C, stroke top+left 1px #3C3C3C, stroke bottom+right 1px #000000. Pressed state inverts the two bevel strokes and scales to 0.96 for 60ms. Value inset: roundRect r=8, fill #000000, 1px #2E2E2E border, numeral in Nunito Sans 700 #C8C8C8 centred. Label tab: small roundRect (r=6, ~72×26) fill #262626 with #3C3C3C top stroke, sitting so its bottom 8px overlap the parent plate's top edge, label in Nunito Sans 600 #B4B4B4 15px. FIRE: 200×72 plate filled with a solid #E46602 (classic-PC treatment — most tappable), 2px #7A3000 inner shadow line at the bottom, label in Press Start 2P 22px #FFFFFF with a 2px #000 offset shadow; 12px dead-zone margin on all sides so it can never be hit by a Move mis-tap. Weapon popup: full-bleed dark overlay, panel with a 3px #A8A8A8 outer bevel and #252525 inner border, interior #000000; rows 84px tall = 56×56 silver icon tile (#ABABAB plate, bevelled) + name in Nunito Sans 26px #FFFFFF; unselected rows above/below fade to #6A6A6A and #3A3A3A to imply a scroll wheel; the selected row gets fill #1A1A00, a 1px #C8C800 border and #FFE000 text. Confirm control is a grey disc (#8A8A8A, bevel ring #4A4A4A) with a thick 2-segment #22C822 check stroked over it. Weapon Shop screen: vertical gradient #000000 → #5D008A, title "WEAPON SHOP" in Press Start 2P silver (#C0C0C0) with hard black shadow, left/right arsenal panels as roundRect r=14 with a 4px border in that player's colour (#B00000, #B26400, etc.) over a transparent interior, centre pool list on a #313131 panel with the current row highlighted #414137 + 1px #C8C800, a "PICK ONE ➜" grey arrow (#9B9B9B) pointing at the active drafter's panel, an "N remaining" counter under the title, and a silver "Quick Pick" plate button at the bottom. In-battle scoreboard: name in Press Start 2P in the player colour with a 2px #000 shadow, a 2px underline in the same colour under the ACTIVE player only, score beneath in #C2C2C2. Off-screen entities get a filled triangle in the owner's colour pinned to the viewport edge (seen as #FF2020 in the reference).

## Motion

Snappy and unceremonious — the game's own tagline is "the fastest game of artillery you will ever play", so nothing may take longer than 400ms except the turn banner. Shell flight is real-time Euler integration at 60fps: gravity g = 0.28 world-px/frame², muzzle speed v = power × 0.145 (so power 100 → 14.5 px/frame; a full-power 45° shot travels ~750 world px). Typical flight 0.8–2.2s; apply a 2.2× timescale while the shell is above the top of the world, and a hard 8s timeout. Shell trail: ring buffer of the last 60 positions stroked as a 1.5px polyline in #E8E8E8 with alpha fading tail-ward from 0.55 to 0. Camera springs to the shell (k=0.12, critically damped, no overshoot), clamped to world bounds; on impact it holds 350ms then eases 400ms cubic-out to the next active tank. Explosion: an expanding ring, radius 0→R over 260ms ease-out, lineWidth 8→1, colour lerping #FFF0C0 → #FF9A20 → #C01000 → transparent, with 3 echo rings offset 60ms each (this reproduces the bright-core-plus-dark-red-rings look in the reference). Canvas filter/blur is banned, so fake the glow with 3 stacked strokes of increasing lineWidth and decreasing alpha. Screen shake on impact: 90ms, amplitude min(10, damage × 0.16)px, exponential decay, applied as a ctx.translate to the BATTLEFIELD ONLY — the deck must never move or fingers miss buttons. Dirt collapse: affected columns lerp to their new height over 140ms ease-out. Damage numbers rise 30px over 900ms with alpha fade, Press Start 2P white. Scores tick up digit-by-digit over 400ms rather than snapping. Buttons: 60ms scale-0.96 plus bevel inversion. Hold-to-repeat on the ± steppers: 380ms delay then 60ms interval accelerating to 20ms after 1.2s. Weapon popup slides up + fades in 160ms. Turn handover: a 700ms banner slides in from the side in the incoming player's colour reading "MARLA'S SHOT · 4th VOLLEY", then out. When the incoming player's seat differs from the outgoing one's, the entire canvas performs a 260ms animated 180° rotation — the phone visibly "turns to face" the next player, which is the single best piece of juice in the whole build.

## Mechanics

GENUINE POCKET TANKS RULES (verified across Wikipedia, StrategyWiki, the App Store listing and the screenshots I viewed):
• There is NO health and NO death. Tanks are never destroyed and are never eliminated. The game is purely a points contest. This is the single most important rule and the most commonly mis-implemented.
• There is NO WIND. Gravity only. This is a defining Pocket Tanks trait versus Scorched Earth/Worms and is why it is learnable in one match. Do not add wind.
• A game is exactly 10 VOLLEYS. A volley = every player takes exactly one turn. Player 1 fires first each volley (fixed order). After volley 10 the highest score wins.
• WEAPON SHOP DRAFT: a pool of exactly 20 weapons is drawn WITH REPLACEMENT from the weapon table — duplicates are common and expected (the reference shop shows "Single Shot / Single Shot", "Crazy Ivan / Crazy Ivan", "Dirt Slinger / Dirt Slinger"). Players alternate picking one at a time starting with P1 until the pool is empty; each ends with exactly 10. The counter reads "N remaining" (6 picked + 5 picked + 9 remaining = 20 in the reference shot). A "Quick Pick" button randomly splits the whole remaining pool evenly and jumps straight to battle. Both arsenals are FULLY VISIBLE to both players — the draft is open information.
• A TURN: the active player may (a) MOVE, (b) pick any unused weapon from their arsenal, (c) set ANGLE and POWER, (d) FIRE. Firing consumes that weapon permanently for the rest of the game.
• MOVE/FUEL: each player has 4 move units for the ENTIRE GAME (not per turn), and may spend at most 1 per turn. Each move shifts the tank ~18 world px left or right and re-snaps it to the surface. A move into a wall steeper than a 30px rise is refused and does not consume fuel. The counter reads 4→0 in the "Move" plate.
• ANGLE 0–180 integer degrees (0 = due right/east, 90 = straight up, 180 = due left/west — the reference shows 113 for a tank firing leftward). POWER 0–100 integer.
• SCORING: each weapon has maxDamage and blastRadius. For every tank within blastRadius of an explosion centre, damage = round(maxDamage × (1 − dist/blastRadius)^1.35), so a direct hit is full value and the edge is 0. Points go to the FIRING player: +damage for each opponent tank hit, and −damage if the firing player's OWN tank is hit. Scores can and do go negative. Multi-explosion weapons resolve each sub-blast independently and stack. Damage numbers pop over each tank as they land.
• TERRAIN is fully destructible: explosions bite craters out of it, dirt weapons add dirt on top. A tank BURIED in dirt has no special rule — it simply cannot be hit until dug out, but its own shell spawns at the barrel tip and instantly collides with the dirt on top of it, so burial is a genuine two-way penalty. This is the core strategic layer (pedestals, bunkers, burying).
• SHELL LIFETIME: destroyed on terrain contact, on direct tank contact (explode at the contact point), on falling below the world floor, or on leaving x < −40 / x > worldW+40. The TOP of the world is OPEN — shells fly off the top of the screen and come back down, and this is normal play.
• TIES: vanilla Pocket Tanks just declares a tie. For this build, break it with sudden-death single-volley rounds where every tied player is granted the same randomly chosen weapon, repeating until broken.
WEAPON TABLE — implement 12–13 (sourced values: Single Shot = 60 damage / small blast, Heatseeker = 40 damage / larger blast; the rest are calibrated by me and should be treated as tunable):
1 Single Shot — 1 shell, dmg 60, radius 34, crater 30. Baseline.
2 Big Shot — 1 shell, dmg 70, radius 62, crater 58, muzzle ×0.9.
3 Scatter Shot — splits at apex into 5 shells over a 22° fan, each dmg 22 / radius 24.
4 Hail Storm — at apex spawns 12 droplets falling straight down across a 140px band, each dmg 9 / radius 14.
5 Dirt Slinger — 0 damage, ADDS a 60px-radius dome of dirt at impact.
6 Mountain Mover — 0 damage, REMOVES a 110px-radius crater.
7 Heatseeker — after 0.5s applies 0.10 px/f² lateral steering toward the nearest enemy tank, but only while that tank is within a 25° cone of the velocity vector; dmg 40, radius 40.
8 Chain Reaction — on impact spawns 4 sequential blasts, each 55px further along the travel direction, 130ms apart, dmg 20 / radius 34 each.
9 Pile Driver — drives a 22px-wide shaft 120px straight down removing dirt; dmg 45, radius 30.
10 Super Star — bursts at apex into 5 star sub-shells that each burst again into 3; ~15 impacts, dmg 8 / radius 16 each.
11 Cruiser — gravity disabled after launch, flies in a dead-straight line at constant speed until it hits something; dmg 50, radius 36.
12 Zapper — instantaneous 6px-wide straight beam from the barrel at the set angle, ignores gravity, dmg 35 to every tank crossed, no crater.
13 Pedestal — 0 damage, raises a 40px-wide, 90px-tall column of dirt directly under the FIRING player's own tank.

## One-phone adaptation

SEATING & ORIENTATION. Phone flat on the table, portrait. Because the core loop has ZERO hidden information — everybody watches every shell fly, and the vanilla weapon shop is open to both players — the phone can stay flat in the middle of the table for the entire game. No privacy screen is needed for normal play. That is the key structural insight and it should drive the whole build. Each player is assigned a SEAT during setup: BOTTOM (0°) or TOP (180°). For a 2-player duel, default P1 = bottom, P2 = top; for 3–8 players default everyone to bottom and let anyone toggle their own seat with a chip on the setup screen. On each handover, if the incoming player's seat differs from the outgoing one's, the ENTIRE canvas renders through ctx.translate(W,H); ctx.rotate(PI) — battlefield and deck together — animated over 260ms. A top-seated player then sees an upright world with the control deck at the physical near edge (the top of the screen), which is exactly where their hands are. CRITICAL IMPLEMENTATION NOTE: every pointer hit-test must be run through the same inverse transform as the render, or all buttons silently break in flipped mode. Store one `flip` boolean, apply it in exactly one place for drawing and one place for input mapping.
LAYOUT (fractions of screen height, portrait ~390×844):
0.000–0.055 volley banner strip, centred, Press Start 2P.
0.055–0.155 scoreboard rail — up to 8 name+score chips as two rows of four (never a horizontal scroller; the active player's chip gets a coloured underline and a 1.08 scale).
0.155–0.620 battlefield viewport (~390×390). The world is wider than the viewport and scrolls (faithful — the real game has "large scrolling battlefields" with edge triangles for off-screen tanks). World width = 900 + 340×N world px.
0.620–1.000 control deck (~320px), four non-overlapping rows:
 Row 1 (56px): full-width WEAPON plate — icon tile + name + "3/8 left". Tap opens the full-screen weapon list overlay.
 Row 2 (120px): AIM PAD — a 320×120 half-dome drag surface; the angular position of the drag sets ANGLE (snapped to whole degrees), the radial distance from the dome's origin sets POWER 0–100. Flanked by four 64×48 stepper chips (angle −/+, power −/+) with hold-to-repeat, because a raw drag alone gives ±3° jitter which destroys artillery. The dome renders a live barrel-angle needle and a power ruler-tick fill.
 Row 3 (72px): MOVE ◀ and ▶ (96×64 each), greyed when fuel = 0, with 4 fuel pips and the numeric readout between them.
 Row 4 (76px): FIRE, 200×72, centred, with a 12px dead-zone margin separating it from Row 3 so a Move mis-tap can never fire.
Every zone is a disjoint rect; nothing overlaps; every tappable target is ≥ 48×48.
SCALING 2–8 PLAYERS. Free-for-all is the default: N tanks spread evenly across the wider world with a minimum separation of 220 world px, turn order = seat order, each player fires exactly once per volley, nobody is ever eliminated so nobody is ever bored out of the game. Splash that catches several tanks scores against each of them separately, and self-damage still subtracts. Optional TEAMS mode for 4/6/8: alternate teams around the circle, team score = sum, friendly fire counts against your own team. VOLLEY COUNT MUST SCALE or an 8-player game runs 80 shots and dies: 2p→10 volleys, 3p→8, 4p→6, 5p→5, 6p→4, 7p→4, 8p→3, keeping every game to ~20–28 total shots (6–9 minutes). Arsenal size = volley count; draft pool size = volleys × N. Draft order is straight alternation for 2 players (faithful to the original) and SNAKE order (1,2,…,N,N,…,2,1) for 3+, because straight repeating order badly favours seat 1 when N is large.
HIDDEN INFORMATION. None in the core loop — say so and exploit it. The one optional variant that needs it is SEALED SHOP: each player drafts behind a pass-the-phone privacy curtain (a full-screen black card reading "PASS TO ▸ MARLA — tap and hold to reveal", requiring a 400ms hold so a bystander glance can't catch it), arsenals hidden from opponents until each weapon is fired. Offer it as a toggle, but ship OPEN SHOP as the default: it keeps the phone flat, keeps everyone watching, and preserves the fast social identity the game is built on.
PASS-THE-PHONE HANDOVER. Between turns, show the 700ms colour banner rather than a blocking "tap to continue" gate — with no hidden information there is nothing to protect, and a gate would kill the pace. Only Sealed Shop uses a real blocking curtain.

## Drawing it with no assets

TERRAIN — the whole look, and it needs no assets. Store a Float32Array `surfaceY[worldW]` (1 entry per world pixel, worldW ≈ 900+340N). Generate with midpoint displacement plus two octaves of hashed value noise, smoothed with a 9-tap box blur, biased so the two ends are lower than the middle. Render the contour terracing by drawing FIVE stacked filled paths, each one the surface polyline offset DOWNWARD by a fixed depth and closed to the bottom of the world — draw deepest first: depth 150+ fill #004400, depth 90 fill #005000, depth 35 fill #007400, depth 4 fill #009C00, depth 0 fill #00D400. Because each band is the same polyline translated in y, the contours follow every cliff and crater exactly like the reference. Decimate to every 3rd world column when building the point list (≈700 points × 5 paths) and only re-tessellate when the heightmap actually changes. Add per-column green jitter via a cheap integer hash (h = x*374761393; jitter = ((h>>16)&15)−8) applied to the band fill by drawing 3–4px-wide vertical slivers on the top two bands only. Add the brushed-streak texture with ~40 hashed vertical lines at rgba(0,0,0,0.06), 2px wide, spanning the deep fill.
DESTRUCTION on a heightmap: for a crater at (cx,cy,r), for each column dx∈[−r,r] let d=√(r²−dx²); if (cy−d) ≤ surfaceY[x] then surfaceY[x] = max(surfaceY[x], cy+d). Dirt-add is the mirror: surfaceY[x] = min(surfaceY[x], cy−d). Animate affected columns to their new value over 140ms. Do NOT attempt caves — see risks.
SKY: one vertical linearGradient across the battlefield rect with stops 0.00 #000000, 0.55 #000000, 0.78 #1A0026, 0.92 #3D005B, 1.00 #5D008A. Plus ~40 stars as 1px white fillRects at hashed positions, twinkling by alpha only (0.35–0.9, sin of time × hashed phase).
TANK: all Path2D primitives. Treads = roundRect(−14,−2,28,5,2) filled #202020 with 6 wheel circles r=1.8 in a 30%-lightened player colour. Hull = a closed trapezoid path (−13,−2)→(13,−2)→(8,−9)→(−8,−9) filled with a vertical linearGradient from lighten(playerColour, 0.45) to darken(playerColour, 0.4). Turret = arc(0,−9,5,PI,2PI) filled with the same gradient shifted up. Barrel = a 14px line from (0,−11) at the aim angle, stroked 3px #101010 then 1.6px #C8C8C8 over it. Whole tank is rotated by atan2(surfaceY[x+6]−surfaceY[x−6], 12) clamped to ±35°, translated to (x, surfaceY[x]).
EXPLOSION: concentric stroked arcs, no filter/blur — 3 stacked strokes per ring at lineWidth ×1, ×2.2, ×4 with alpha 1.0, 0.35, 0.14 to fake glow, colour lerped through #FFF0C0 → #FF9A20 → #C01000. Plus a solid #000000 disc drawn into the crater once the ring passes it.
WEAPON ICONS: 24×24 inline SVG path data fed to `new Path2D(d)`, flat two-tone, seated on a 56×56 silver bevel tile (fill #ABABAB, 1px #E8E8E8 top-left, 1px #565656 bottom-right). Twelve paths total, e.g. star = "M12 2l3 7h7l-5.5 4.5L18 21l-6-4-6 4 1.5-7.5L2 9h7z", down-triangle = "M3 5h18l-9 14z", bolt = "M13 2L4 14h6l-1 8 9-12h-6z", crosshair = circle+cross strokes, mountain = "M2 20l6-11 4 6 3-4 7 9z", mushroom cluster = 5 overlapping circles. Colour each glyph per weapon (#FF3030 scatter, #40C0FF hail, #FFD000 star, #E08000 dirt, #C060FF pile driver).
UI PLATES: a single `plate(x,y,w,h,r,pressed)` helper using ctx.roundRect + two half-perimeter strokes (top-left #3C3C3C, bottom-right #000000, swapped when pressed) covers every control. The Angle arch is the same helper with the top corner radius set to h/2. The Power ruler is a black inset plus a for-loop of 26 1px #3A3A3A ticks plus a clipped left-to-right linearGradient fill #7A0000 → #C41010.
TEXT: preload Cormorant/etc. is unnecessary — link only the two Google Fonts families (Press Start 2P, Nunito Sans) and give both a real fallback stack ("Press Start 2P", ui-monospace, monospace / "Nunito Sans", system-ui, sans-serif). Draw the hard drop-shadow on names manually by filling the string twice with a 2–3px offset in #000000 first.
PERFORMANCE: one full-screen canvas, one rAF loop, no per-frame allocations in the shell/particle update (use preallocated typed arrays and a free-list for explosion rings and damage-number popups). Zero images, zero remote requests beyond the two font stylesheets, zero emoji.

## Risks

- A heightmap cannot represent caves, tunnels or overhangs, so Digger/Drillers/Ground Hog-style weapons must be cut outright. State this up front so the build doesn't half-implement them. Craters, pedestals, bunkers and burial all still work correctly on a heightmap, which covers the real strategic loop.
- Redrawing five full-width terrain band paths every frame will stutter on low-end phones. Decimate the polyline to every 3rd world column and only re-tessellate the shared point list when the heightmap actually mutates — otherwise reuse the cached Path2D objects.
- The 180° canvas rotation for opposite-seated players inverts touch coordinates. Every hit-test must run through the exact inverse of the render transform, applied in one single place. This is by far the most likely bug and it fails silently — all buttons appear dead for the top-seated player.
- Angle/power set by drag alone jitters by ±3°, which is fatal for artillery where ranging in on a target is the entire skill. The drag pad MUST be paired with hold-to-repeat ± steppers and snapped to whole degrees, or the game feels random rather than skilful.
- 8 players × 10 volleys is 80 shots (~25 minutes) and the game dies mid-session. Volley count must scale down with player count (2p→10 down to 8p→3) so every game lands at 20–28 total shots.
- Press Start 2P is extremely wide; names like GUILDENSTERN will overflow an 8-chip scoreboard rail. Cap entered names at 7 characters and measureText-and-shrink each chip, and never set weapon names in it.
