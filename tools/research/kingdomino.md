# kingdomino — design brief

*Compiled from reference images the researcher downloaded and viewed.*

## Art direction

I viewed the box cover, the official rules scoring diagram (flat top-down photo of real printed tiles), a retail components photo, and four screenshots of the licensed digital edition. The physical tiles are FLAT, top-down, fully-saturated storybook illustration printed edge-to-edge on thick cardboard: each 1x2 domino is two hard-edged squares of pure flat terrain colour (no bevel, no gloss) carrying tiny hand-painted vignettes — a brown windmill on wheat, round broccoli-like tree clumps on forest, a cream hut with a burnt-orange roof plus a rowboat and red fish on the cyan lake, a red-orange dragon on the chartreuse grassland, a wooden mine cart spilling gold nuggets on the near-black mine. Colour is loud and childlike: wheat is a shrieking egg-yolk #F9D306, water a clean cerulean #1D93D2, grassland an acid chartreuse #C6CD2F, forest a deep bottle green #2E5A2A, swamp a chalky mud-beige #C5BD9F, mine almost black #221F1B — six hues chosen so no two are confusable at a glance, which is the entire reason the game reads across a table. The official diagram sits these tiles on a pale sky field #A4DBF0 and outlines each scored territory with a chunky WHITE DASHED stroke, then labels it with chalky hand-lettered "2x4 =8" callouts (crowns x squares) and curved white arrows. The gold crown is the brand emblem and appears everywhere at every scale: a small three-point crown, warm gold body with a red-brown keyline, always sitting in the lower-right of its square. The digital edition adds the UI vocabulary I will steal: parchment/white rounded panels with grey rims, a crimson notched ribbon nameplate over each player card, mini 5x5 grid thumbnails, score pills with a crown, and a gold pill CTA with a darker gold bottom rim.

- `#2E5A2A` — forest terrain (deep bottle green, sampled)
- `#1D93D2` — lake terrain (cerulean, sampled)
- `#C5BD9F` — swamp terrain (chalky mud beige, sampled)
- `#221F1B` — mine terrain (near-black charcoal, sampled)
- `#F2C438` — crown gold — body fill + all score accents
- `#8A4A12` — crown/tile keyline, wood, dark outline on everything
- `#F3E4C0` — parchment panel fill (HUD, handoff card, buttons)
- `#0F2C3D` — table felt background (deep dusk teal, top of gradient)
- `#D2412F` — castle roof red / illegal-move error flash
- `#A4DBF0` — sky accent (round banner, water highlight, empty-cell tint)

## Typography

Use exactly three of the eight. DM SERIF DISPLAY for the wordmark, the round banner ("ROUND 7 OF 12"), and the winner line — its high-contrast heavy stems and sharp serifs are the closest available match to the Kingdomino logo's regal storybook lettering, and unlike Cormorant Garamond it stays solid and legible at phone sizes against saturated tiles. BEBAS NEUE for every numeral: the 1-48 tile rank badges, crown counts, the "4 x 3 = 12" scoring callouts, running totals, and round counters — tall, condensed, all-caps, high impact, and it is a near-direct substitute for the chunky white outlined numerals the digital edition uses in its scoring popup, while packing into narrow badges without shrinking. NUNITO SANS (600 for labels, 800 for buttons and player names) for all remaining UI text — its rounded humanist terminals carry the friendly family-game tone of the box art and it is the most legible option at 12-14px. Reject Inter (too neutral/corporate for a fantasy kingdom), Space Mono and Space Grotesk (wrong era, technical), and Press Start 2P (pixel-retro, actively fights the painterly illustration).

## Shape language

The atom is the SQUARE and its doubling, the 2:1 domino — every shape in the game derives from that ratio. Tiles are hard-edged squares with only a 3-4px corner radius and a heavy dark keyline: die-cut cardboard, never glassy, never soft. Terrain art inside a square is built only from filled shapes with dark keylines — circles for tree canopies and gold nuggets, triangles for pine tops, sails, roofs and fish, trapezoids for the windmill tower and the meeple body, quadratic arcs for water ripples and boat hulls — no hairline-only icons anywhere. UI panels are rounded rects at r=14 filled parchment with a 2px #8A4A12 rim and a 1px inner cream highlight, reading as an illuminated manuscript card. Buttons are pills (r = height/2) faked into 3D by drawing a 3px-offset darker duplicate underneath, exactly like the digital edition's gold CTA. The player token is a meeple silhouette traced as one path: circle head sitting on a trapezoid body with two arm nubs and a flat base, filled with the player colour and stroked 25% darker. The three-point crown is the recurring emblem and is re-drawn at 10px, 18px and 96px from the same path.

## UI chrome

Translate the digital edition's chrome literally. Player cards: a rounded parchment rect with a CRIMSON NOTCHED RIBBON header (a rect with triangular notches cut from both ends) carrying the player name in Nunito Sans 800 white, a circular avatar well on the left holding the meeple silhouette in the player colour, a mini grid thumbnail on the right (each cell a 4px flat terrain swatch, crowns as 2px gold dots), and a white score pill with a crown glyph and a Bebas Neue number. Draft lane: a vertical list of rows, each row a full-width 2:1 domino rendered at real fidelity, with a wooden rank badge (rounded rect #6B4B2A, Bebas numeral in cream) pinned to its left edge and, once claimed, a 3px outer ring in the claimant's colour plus their meeple chip seated on the tile — this ring-plus-pawn is exactly how the app shows ownership. Board frame: a 10px wood-brown border #6B4B2A with a lighter #8B6438 bevel line along the top/left and a #3E2A16 line along the bottom/right; empty cells are parchment #E7D9B4 on a 1px #C6B489 grid. Territory outlines during scoring are the box's WHITE DASHED stroke (3px, dash [9,7], animated marching at 18px/s) with the callout in a small dark translucent pill. Round header is a sky-blue #A4DBF0 banner with a gold hairline and DM Serif Display text. Paging/rotate controls are circular parchment buttons with a dark chevron or a hand-drawn arc-with-arrowhead glyph.

## Motion

Chunky, weighty and physical — cardboard being slapped onto a table, never floaty and never longer than ~550ms. Commit a domino: it scales 1.14 -> 0.98 -> 1.0 with easeOutBack over 180ms, throws a two-ring dust puff (two expanding stroked rounded rects, alpha 0.35 -> 0, 260ms), and shakes the whole board 6px damped over 150ms. Illegal tap: 3-cycle horizontal shake at +/-5px over 220ms plus the target cell flashes rgba(210,65,47,0.5). Legal anchor cells breathe a gold inner stroke, alpha oscillating 0.25-0.60 on a 1.6s sine, all cells in phase so it reads as one pulse. Claiming a tile: the player's meeple chip arcs from their HUD to the chosen draft row along a quadratic bezier over 320ms with a slight spin, landing with a single gold ring that expands and fades in 240ms. New round: the N dominoes slide in from the top edge staggered 60ms apart, 240ms easeOutCubic each, entering at 3 degrees of rotation and settling to 0. Seat flip: the world layer rotates 180 degrees over 520ms easeInOutCubic entirely hidden behind the handoff card wiping across. Scoring reveal: territories light one at a time, 200ms apart, each popping its "squares x crowns = pts" callout from 1.25 -> 1.0 while the running total counts up by integers at ~26/s (never eased digits, and capped so one region never takes more than 700ms). One global time base; only easeOutCubic, easeOutBack and easeInOutCubic. Every glow is faked with 3 concentric strokes of decreasing alpha or a radial gradient — the canvas filter/blur property is forbidden.

## Mechanics

COMPONENTS. 48 dominoes, each 1x2 = two terrain squares. 96 squares distributed exactly: WHEAT 26 (21 with 0 crowns, 5 with 1); FOREST 22 (16 with 0, 6 with 1); LAKE 18 (12 with 0, 6 with 1); GRASSLAND 14 (10 with 0, 2 with 1, 2 with 2); SWAMP 10 (6 with 0, 2 with 1, 2 with 2); MINE 6 (1 with 0, 1 with 1, 3 with 2, 1 with 3). Total 39 crowns. Every domino carries a rank 1-48 on its back; ranks are assigned so higher rank = more crowns / rarer terrain. Each player also gets one 1x1 CASTLE tile and, in 2-player, two king pawns instead of one.
SETUP. 4 players: all 48 dominoes. 3 players: remove 12 at random, use 36. 2 players: remove 24 at random, use 24, and each player takes 2 kings. Every player places their castle as the seed square of their own kingdom.
LANE. Each round, draw K dominoes face down where K = total number of kings in play (4p=4, 3p=3, 2p=4), stand them rank-side up, sort ASCENDING by rank top to bottom, then flip to the terrain side. This is the lane.
DRAFT ORDER. Round 1: kings are placed onto the lane in RANDOM order. Every later round: order is the position of each king in the PREVIOUS lane, position 1 (lowest rank) first. A king may only be placed on an unclaimed lane slot; one king per domino. Taking a low-numbered (weak) tile buys you an earlier pick next round — that tension is the whole game.
TURN SEQUENCE. Round 1 is CLAIM-ONLY. Rounds 2..12, in order, each king does both actions: (1) PLACE the domino that king claimed last round into its owner's kingdom; (2) CLAIM one free domino in the new lane. After the last lane (round 12) is fully claimed there is no new lane, so a final PLACE-ONLY pass runs in the last lane's order. Each player therefore places exactly 12 dominoes; 12 dominoes x 2 squares + 1 castle = exactly 25 squares = a full 5x5.
PLACEMENT LEGALITY. Three conditions, all required. (a) Both squares land on empty cells. (b) At least ONE of the two squares is orthogonally adjacent to a square of IDENTICAL terrain already in the kingdom, OR orthogonally adjacent to the castle square — the castle is WILD on all four sides and accepts any terrain. The domino's own two halves touching each other never counts as a connection. Diagonals never count. (c) After placement, the bounding box of ALL occupied cells including the castle must not exceed 5 rows and 5 columns. The castle is NOT required to be centred; the kingdom may grow in any direction, but the moment a placement would make the extent 6 wide or 6 tall it is illegal. Implement on a 9x9 array with the castle at (4,4) and test min/max row/col extents after each candidate placement.
DISCARD. If and only if a player has NO legal placement for their held domino in ANY cell and ANY of the 4 rotations, that domino is discarded and scores nothing, leaving a permanent hole. A player may NEVER choose to discard when a legal placement exists. Placed dominoes can never be moved.
SCORING. Flood-fill orthogonally over same-terrain squares to find TERRITORIES. The castle square belongs to no territory, scores nothing, and does not bridge two territories. Each territory scores (number of squares) x (total crowns inside that territory). A territory with zero crowns scores 0 no matter how large; a lone square with 3 crowns scores 3. Sum all territories. Display the callout as "crowns x squares = pts" to match the official diagram.
BONUSES (official variants, both ON by default). HARMONY: +5 if the kingdom has no empty cells, i.e. all 12 dominoes were placed and nothing was discarded. MIDDLE KINGDOM: +10 if the castle sits in the exact centre of the completed 5x5 — enforce as bounding box exactly 5x5 AND castle at its centre cell.
TIE-BREAK. 1) Largest single territory by square count. 2) Most crowns in total. 3) Still tied: shared victory.
MIGHTY DUEL (2-player variant). All 48 dominoes, 2 kings each, 12 lanes of 4, 24 placements each, kingdom is 7x7 (49 = 24 dominoes x 2 + castle). Middle Kingdom becomes castle at the centre of a full 7x7.

## One-phone adaptation

CRITICAL INSIGHT: Kingdomino has ZERO hidden information. The lane is face-up, every kingdom is face-up, only the shuffled draw pile is unknown. So NO privacy screen is ever needed, and the phone should lie FLAT ON THE TABLE and stay there — players reach in rather than pass. This is what makes it a great one-phone game.
SEATING. Players sit along the two LONG edges of the flat phone, like a domino lane, up to 4 per side for 8 players. At setup each player taps SIDE A (facing up-screen) or SIDE B (facing down-screen) on a small diagram, picks a colour and a preset name (tap to edit; presets avoid keyboard passing). Do NOT attempt 4-way 90-degree rotation: a portrait phone cannot give a side-seated player a usable input band without collapsing the board, whereas a 180-degree flip maps the layout exactly onto itself and wastes zero pixels. Left/right stragglers just sit at an end and tilt their head.
ROTATION. One world layer holds everything and rotates 180 degrees about screen centre when the active player's side changes; it does not rotate when consecutive players share a side. The flip (520ms) is always hidden behind a full-screen HANDOFF CARD that wipes across carrying the next player's name and meeple in their colour, tapped anywhere to begin. Same-side handoffs get only a 400ms colour banner. A tiny non-rotating "active side" dot stays pinned in one screen corner so anyone who looked away can see whose turn it is without reading upside-down text.
LAYOUT (portrait, described for SIDE A; SIDE B is this rotated 180). Top 72px: opponent roster strip — horizontally packed mini cards with meeple, name, live score pill, 5x5 thumbnail. Next ~250px: DRAFT LANE, a vertical list of N rows, each row a real 2:1 domino at row height clamp(44, 300/N, 74)px with the wooden rank badge on the left and the claimant's ring+chip on the right. For N >= 6 the lane switches to a 2-column grid of compact dominoes so it never scrolls and the board never shrinks below 300px. Centre ~374px: the KINGDOM GRID, a square, so rotation is footprint-free. Bottom ~150px: ACTION BAR — the held domino docked at real size, a large circular ROTATE button, a CONFIRM check, and a DISCARD button that is disabled and greyed unless the engine proves zero legal placements exist. Input zones never overlap: lane taps only during CLAIM sub-phase, grid taps only during PLACE sub-phase, and the other zone is dimmed to 45% and hit-test-disabled.
PLACEMENT INPUT (two taps, no drag, safe on a shared phone). On entering PLACE, precompute every legal (anchor cell, rotation) pair and pulse each legal anchor with a gold inner stroke. Tap a legal cell: the full 2-square ghost renders at 85% alpha with the leading square ringed. Tap the SAME cell again: cycle to the next legal rotation at that cell. Tap CONFIRM or double-tap to commit. Tapping an illegal cell shakes and flashes red but never commits. This makes mis-taps recoverable, which matters when a stranger's finger is over your kingdom.
TURN LOOP. Handoff card -> PLACE your held domino -> CLAIM a lane row (tap row, meeple arcs over, auto-advance) -> handoff. Round 1 skips PLACE; the final pass skips CLAIM. Total handoffs = 13N (52 for 4p, 104 for 8p) at roughly 8-12s each.
SCALING TO 5-8 PLAYERS. Keep 12 rounds, 1 king each, lane of N. Generate a pool of 12N dominoes by scaling the official 96-square distribution by k = 12N/48 (1.25 / 1.5 / 1.75 / 2), pairing squares so ~40% of dominoes are single-terrain and crowned squares are spread, then sorting the whole pool by value = crowns*100 + terrain-rarity weight and renumbering 1..12N so the draft tension survives. Offer a QUICK REALM mode for big groups: 3x3 kingdom, 4 rounds, 4 dominoes each (9 = 4x2 + castle) — the only other grid size that divides cleanly; 4x4 is impossible and must not be offered. 2-player offers standard 5x5 (2 kings, 6 lanes) or Mighty Duel 7x7 (2 kings, 12 lanes).
SCORING PHASE. Reveal one player at a time, rotating the world to THAT player's side first, marching white dashed outlines around each territory in turn with its "crowns x squares" callout, then the bonuses, then the total. Ends on a compact ranked result card for THIS game only — no in-game leaderboard screen.

## Drawing it with no assets

ZERO assets. Every square is drawn by one function drawTerrain(ctx, x, y, size, terrain, seed) where seed is a deterministic hash of (tileId, halfIndex) so texture is stable across every redraw and across the mini thumbnails. Each terrain = base vertical linear gradient (2 stops, the palette hex to a 12% darker shade) + procedural texture + optional motif on crowned squares.
WHEAT #F9D306 -> #E0B403: 32-40 short near-vertical strokes, 1px, #C99A00, each leaning +/-8 degrees, plus 6 dark specks. Motif: windmill = brown trapezoid tower #8A5A2B + 4 thin cream rects rotated 90 degrees apart from a hub circle.
GRASSLAND #C6CD2F -> #A3AC22: 20 three-stroke chevron tufts in #7E9A17 + 8 flower dots alternating #E8524A and #FFFFFF. Motif: sheep = white ellipse body, dark #3A3226 head circle, 4 one-px legs; or a red-orange dragon = 3 chained triangles + a curved tail bezier.
FOREST #2E5A2A -> #1F401C: 9-14 canopies, each 2-3 overlapping circles r=6..10 filled #2E6B26 with a lighter #4F9440 arc on the top-left quadrant and a 2x5 #5A3A1F trunk rect. Motif: a taller stacked-triangle pine.
LAKE #1D93D2 -> #1573AE: 5-7 horizontal quadratic wave arcs in rgba(255,255,255,0.35) at varying widths + 2-3 fish = filled triangle plus tail chevron in #E8524A. Motif: fishing hut = cream #F0E2C0 box with a #E2622B triangular roof on 2 stilt lines, or a boat = arc hull + mast line + sail triangle.
SWAMP #C5BD9F -> #A79C77: 8-12 closed-bezier mottle blobs alternating #8B7B52 and #E4DCC0 + 3 reed strokes bending one way. Motif: bubbling pool = 3 concentric ellipses in decreasing alpha with 3 small circles rising.
MINE #221F1B -> #141210: 6-9 angular rock facets (random 3-4 point polygons) in #3B352E with a #554C40 highlight along the upper edge + 5-8 nuggets = circles r=2.5 in #F2C438 each with a #FFF0A8 highlight dot. Motif: mine cart = #7A4A22 box, 2 near-black wheel circles, a gold pile of 5 circles.
CASTLE SQUARE: the player colour at 35% over #7BA644, then a gold castle — central keep rect + two flanking tower rects in #E8C24A with #C99A2E right-side shading, crenellations as a row of 4x5 notch rects along each top edge, a #D2412F conical roof triangle on the keep with a 1px pennant line, and a dark #4A3010 arched door (rect + half-circle).
CROWN PATH (the emblem, one function, any size): moveTo(0,10) lineTo(0,3) lineTo(2.6,6) lineTo(5,0) lineTo(7.4,6) lineTo(10,3) lineTo(10,10) close; fill with a vertical gradient #FFD955 -> #E09A16, stroke #8A4A12 at 1.2 scaled px, add a #C77A18 band rect (0,7.6,10,2.4) and three #FFF2B0 jewel dots at the peaks. Draw at 18px in the square's lower-right; for 2-3 crowns lay them in a row behind a rgba(0,0,0,0.28) rounded pill so gold reads on wheat as well as on mine.
MEEPLE PATH: circle head r=3.4 at (5,3), body path (1.2,12) -> (2.4,7.6) -> (3.6,6.6) -> (6.4,6.6) -> (7.6,7.6) -> (8.8,12) close, plus two arm nubs; fill player colour, stroke the colour darkened 25%.
TILE CHROME: every square gets a 1.5px inner rgba(0,0,0,0.22) border and a 1px top/left rgba(255,255,255,0.18) highlight. A placed domino gets a 2.5px #2A211A outer stroke around its 2-square footprint plus a FAKE drop shadow made of 3 concentric rounded-rect strokes at alpha 0.20/0.12/0.06 offset (0,2) — canvas filter/blur is forbidden.
BACKGROUND: vertical gradient #0F2C3D -> #071A26, then a procedural felt weave (two sets of 1px lines at 3px pitch, one horizontal one vertical, rgba(255,255,255,0.035)), then a centred radial gradient white at 6% alpha as a table light, then a 4-stop radial vignette to black at 22%. Board frame is a rounded rect r=8 in #6B4B2A with a #8B6438 top/left bevel line and a #3E2A16 bottom/right line. Empty cells are #E7D9B4 with a 1px #C6B489 grid.
No document.createElement and no getBoundingClientRect: size the canvas from window.innerWidth/innerHeight * devicePixelRatio, take pointer coords straight from touch/pointer clientX/clientY, and hit-test by inverse-transforming through the same rotation matrix used to render. Load the three Google Fonts via a link the host already permits, and set explicit fallbacks ("DM Serif Display", Georgia, serif / "Bebas Neue", Impact, sans-serif / "Nunito Sans", system-ui, sans-serif).

## Risks

- Terrain confusability at ~62px cells and for colourblind players — wheat vs grassland (yellow vs yellow-green) and grassland vs forest are the dangerous pairs. Mitigate with sharply different texture GRAMMAR per terrain (wheat = vertical strokes, grass = chevron tufts + flower dots, forest = round canopies) so shape carries the signal, plus an optional single-letter corner glyph.
- Draft lane vs board space at 7-8 players: 8 rows plus a 5x5 board plus HUD does not fit 844px at comfortable sizes. Must switch the lane to a 2-column compact grid at N>=6 and floor the board at 300px; getting this threshold wrong makes the lane unreadable or the board untappable.
- Scoring engine correctness is silent-failure territory: the castle must belong to no territory and must NOT bridge two same-terrain regions across itself, discarded dominoes must leave permanent holes that kill Harmony, and Middle Kingdom must require a full 5x5 bounding box with the castle at its centre. An off-by-one in the flood fill or the bounding-box test breaks the game invisibly.
- Turn fatigue: 13N handoffs means 104 turns at 8 players. Every turn must be two taps maximum, the handoff card must be dismissible by a tap anywhere, and a Quick Realm 3x3 / 4-round mode must be offered for big groups.
- The 180-degree flip disorients anyone who glanced away. Never rotate without the handoff card fully covering the transition, and keep one small non-rotating active-side indicator pinned to a screen corner.
- Placement ambiguity — which half of the domino lands where. Precompute all legal (cell, rotation) pairs, pulse only legal anchors, always show the full 2-square ghost with the leading square ringed, and cycle rotations by re-tapping the same cell rather than relying on a separate button being noticed.
