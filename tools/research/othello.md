# othello — design brief

*Compiled from reference images the researcher downloaded and viewed.*

## Art direction

I downloaded and actually viewed five photos: (1) an extreme close-up of a bright plastic Othello board — saturated kelly green field ~#1FA83C–#37A144 with hairline near-black grid lines, thick pucks whose white faces read warm cream #F5EFCF and whose black faces pick up green bounce (#002900 in shadow); (2) a tournament folding board — charcoal-black stepped bezel with small brass hinge plates on the side rails, jade felt field (photographed under flash at #97D5A1, true value nearer #6FA882), grid lines that are a DARKER DESATURATED GREEN, not black, and four small dark star dots at the intersections two squares in from each corner; (3) the classic Mattel/Tsukuda set — glossy black moulded tray with two clear plastic sliding gutter lids holding disc stacks, "othello" embossed in lowercase on the inner black lip, deep teal-green FELT field sampled at #256766–#2A745C, thin dark grid, and discs whose defining feature is a CREAM/IVORY SIDE WALL visible as a crescent under every face (#D8C9A0-ish) — that ivory rim is the single most important detail because it is what sells the flip; (4) a cheap folding magnetic travel set, harsher kelly green with heavy black grid; (5) a modern Megahouse set — glossy yellow-green field (~#8FCB4F) with WHITE grid lines and a recessed circular well moulded into every cell, matte discs, black frame, clear gutters. Synthesis for the rebuild: commit to the Mattel/Tsukuda register — deep green felt, near-black incised grid, four star dots, glossy black bezel with a brass plaque, thick two-sided discs with ivory side walls and short soft contact shadows. Light source is fixed at upper-left ~34%/42%, which drives every gradient. For 3–8 players keep the identical physical language and only swap the disc face colour: Ivory #F4F2EA, Onyx #191A18, Crimson #D93A32, Azure #3B82E8, Amber #F0B429, Violet #9B5DE5, Magenta #E8459B, Sky #48CFE0 — all keep the same ivory side wall so the whole set still reads as one moulded product.

## Typography

Bebas Neue for all numerals and display: score counts, "BLACK TO PLAY", the engraved OTHELLO wordmark on the bezel. It is condensed all-caps with flat moulded terminals — it matches the injection-plastic, tightly-tracked lettering on the real game box and packs huge legible digits into a thin HUD strip, which matters because a portrait phone leaves only ~190px of chrome per side. Space Grotesk for everything secondary: player names, button labels, rule/pass explainer cards, settings. Its neo-grotesque forms with slightly cut terminals read cleanly at 11–13px AND stay legible when the entire top strip is rotated 180° for the player across the table, where a humanist face would soften into mush. Reject Cormorant Garamond and DM Serif Display (literary; Othello is a 1971 moulded plastic object, not a book), reject Press Start 2P (wrong era and unreadable upside-down at small sizes), reject Inter/Nunito Sans (correct but characterless — Bebas gives the plaque-engraved feel the bezel needs).

## Shape language

Everything is a circle inside a square inside a rounded rectangle. Hard 90° grid of perfect squares; perfect circles for discs at 0.42 × cell radius (discs never touch the grid lines, leaving a ~8% green gutter, exactly as in the photos). The bezel is a rounded rect at radius 18px; the felt playfield is a rounded rect at radius 6px inset 14px inside it. No diagonals anywhere except the star dots (tiny circles) and the flip animation's elliptical squash. Discs have real thickness — they are a capsule, not a flat circle: top ellipse + ivory side wall band ~0.13r tall. HUD chips are pills (fully rounded rects, radius = half height) containing a mini-disc. Buttons are rounded rects at radius 12. The whole visual grammar is "moulded plastic with 1–2mm fillets everywhere" — nothing is razor-sharp, nothing is organic.

## UI chrome

The board is framed by a glossy black bezel that IS the chrome: rounded rect filled with a top-left→bottom-right linear gradient #3A4247→#0D1013, inset by a second rounded rect of flat #14171A, then a 3px rgba(255,255,255,0.22) stroke on the top and left edges only and rgba(0,0,0,0.6) on bottom/right to give the moulded gloss. Two brass hinge plaques (4×14 rounded rects, gradient #C9A227→#8A6D14) sit at the left and right mid-edges. The word OTHELLO is engraved into the bottom bezel in Bebas Neue 11px with 4px letter-spacing, drawn twice — rgba(255,255,255,0.10) at y+1 then rgba(0,0,0,0.55) at y — for a free intaglio effect. Outside the bezel: two HUD strips on flat #14181A panels (rounded rect, 1px rgba(255,255,255,0.07) top highlight). Each strip holds (a) a turn banner in Bebas 22px whose background is a 14%-alpha wash of the active player's colour, (b) a horizontal rail of player chips — pill, mini-disc at r=11 rendered by the same disc routine, Bebas 30px count, Space Grotesk 10px name — with the active chip carrying a 2px stroke in its own colour plus a glow faked as three concentric strokes at alpha 0.18/0.10/0.05 (canvas filter/blur is banned), (c) a 10px-tall stacked territory bar showing every player's share of occupied cells as a proportional segment, and (d) a full-width CONFIRM button. Legal-move hints are hollow rings on the felt, not filled cells. The last move is marked by a thin 2px ring in the mover's colour around that disc, persisting until the next commit. No modal dialogs except the pass/no-move explainer card and the end-of-game card.

## Motion

Weighty plastic, never bouncy-cartoon. Drop: the ghost disc solidifies and scales 1.15→1.00 over 140ms on cubic ease-out while its contact shadow expands from 60%→100% — one small overshoot, no wobble. THE FLIP is the whole game's signature and must be a physical rotation, not a colour swap: animate θ from 0→π over 220ms with an ease-in-out sine, draw the top face as an ellipse of rx=r, ry=r·|cos θ|, swap the face colour exactly at θ=π/2, and as |cos θ| shrinks let the ivory side wall grow beneath the ellipse (wall height = thickness · (1−|cos θ|·0.35)) so the disc visibly turns on edge and shows its cream rim mid-flip — that ivory flash is the beat players feel. Flips CASCADE outward: sort captured discs by Chebyshev distance from the placed disc and stagger start times by 45ms per ring, all eight directions running in parallel, so a 12-disc capture ripples like a wave over ~700ms. Score numerals integer-lerp and the territory bar segments tween on the same clock, so you literally watch territory transfer. Legal-move rings breathe scale 1.00↔1.06 at a 1.4s period, alpha 0.30. Capturing a corner fires a 400ms gold radial pulse from that corner plus three 1px spark lines — corners are permanent, so celebrate them. On every commit the entire board group settles: translate +2px then back over 90ms. Turn handoff sweeps a full-width colour wipe across both strips in 260ms. Everything is deterministic and time-based (ms elapsed), never frame-count-based.

## Mechanics

TWO-PLAYER MODE IS STRICT OFFICIAL OTHELLO (World Othello Federation rules). Board 8×8. Setup with 0-indexed [row][col], row 0 at top: (3,3)=White, (4,4)=White, (3,4)=Black, (4,3)=Black — i.e. d4/e5 White, e4/d5 Black. BLACK ALWAYS MOVES FIRST. Black's four legal opening moves are d3, c4, f5, e6, all equivalent by symmetry.
LEGAL MOVE: cell must be EMPTY, and in at least one of the 8 directions (dr,dc)∈{-1,0,1}²\{(0,0)} there must be a run of one or more CONTIGUOUS opponent discs starting at the adjacent cell and terminated by a disc of the mover's own colour, with no empty cell and no own-colour disc inside the run. Pseudocode: i=r+dr, j=c+dc, n=0; while inBounds && board[i][j]==opponent { n++; i+=dr; j+=dc } ; direction captures n iff n>0 && inBounds(i,j) && board[i][j]==mover. Move is legal iff total captured ≥ 1. Placing a disc that captures nothing is ILLEGAL — there is no "just place it" in 2P.
FLIP: place the disc, then flip EXACTLY the discs in every capturing run. Rules that matter: (a) you may not skip over your own disc to reach an opponent run; (b) only discs in a direct unbroken straight line from the placed disc flip — nothing else on the board changes; (c) flips DO NOT CHAIN — you do not re-evaluate brackets created by the newly flipped discs; (d) ALL outflanked discs must be flipped, even when it hurts you — partial capture is not allowed; (e) all directions resolve simultaneously from the pre-move board state.
PASS: if a player has no legal move, the turn is forfeited and the opponent moves again. Passing is mandatory and only permitted when no legal move exists.
END: when neither player can move — board full (64), or a player has been wiped to zero discs (they can never move again, so end immediately), or mutual block with empty cells remaining. Maximum 60 moves. Winner = more discs of their colour showing. 32–32 is a draw.
SCORING: display raw disc counts on the board. For the platform score use the tournament convention — if the game ends before the board is full, all remaining EMPTY squares are credited to the winner (33–28 with 3 empties scores 36–28). Disc supply is unlimited in software (the physical rule is that a player out of discs takes them from the opponent).
THREE-TO-EIGHT PLAYER MODE (Rolit-style, stated exactly): board is 8×8 for N=3–4, 10×10 for N=5–6, 12×12 for N=7–8 (≈16–18 cells per player either way). Setup: for N=2 hard-code the classic centre. For N≥3 give every player exactly two starting discs placed point-symmetrically about the board centre — for player index i (0-based, seat order) compute angles θ=2πi/N and θ+π on a circle of radius 1.5 cells (N≤4) or 2.5 cells (N≥5) from the centre point, snap each to the nearest cell, and spiral outward to the nearest empty cell on collision. This is rotationally symmetric by construction so no seat is favoured. Turn order is fixed clockwise around the seat ring.
CAPTURE (N≥3): identical bracketing rule, except a run may contain discs of ANY colours other than the mover's, mixed freely, and every disc in a capturing run flips to the mover's colour — including third parties' discs. Same eight directions, same no-chaining, same must-flip-all.
FALLBACK PLACEMENT (N≥3 only): if and only if you have NO capturing move anywhere, you MUST instead place a disc on any empty cell that is orthogonally or diagonally adjacent to at least one occupied cell. This flips nothing. It guarantees the board keeps filling and nobody is ever skipped, so in 3–8P there are effectively no passes. A player reduced to zero discs on the board is NOT eliminated — the fallback lets them re-enter.
END (N≥3): board full, or a whole round passes with no player able to place at all. Most discs of your colour wins; exact ties are a shared win. Report every player's final count.

## One-phone adaptation

Othello is PERFECT INFORMATION — there is zero hidden state, so this game needs no privacy screen and no forced pass-the-phone. That is the design's advantage: the phone lies FLAT on the table for the whole game and everyone watches the same board. Exploit it rather than bolting on a hand-hiding flow.
SEATING: players sit along the two long sides of the phone. At setup a "claim your seat" screen shows a phone outline with N colour chips; each player taps a chip and taps TOP or BOTTOM to say which side they are on. Their HUD chip then lives on that strip. Seat order for turns is fixed clockwise regardless of which side they chose.
ORIENTATION: the 8×8 board carries no text — only grid, star dots and discs — so it is orientation-neutral and NEVER rotates (rotating it every turn across 60 moves would be nauseating). Instead the two HUD strips are mirror copies: the bottom strip draws upright, the top strip draws with ctx.translate(cx,cy); ctx.rotate(Math.PI); ctx.translate(-cx,-cy) around the same single draw function. One code path, two orientations. Both strips always show the same information, so the player across the table reads their own upright copy.
LAYOUT (390×844 reference, scale proportionally; keep the board square and centred on taller screens by growing the strips): TOP STRIP y∈[0,196] rotated 180° — CONFIRM button x∈[24,366] y∈[12,76] placed hard against the top edge so the far player's thumb lands on it naturally, turn banner y∈[86,124], player chip rail + territory bar y∈[130,190]. BOARD x∈[4,386] y∈[206,588], 382px square (8×8 → 47.75px cells; 10×10 → 38.2px; 12×12 → 31.8px). BOTTOM STRIP y∈[598,844] upright — turn banner y∈[600,638], chip rail + bar y∈[644,704], CONFIRM x∈[24,366] y∈[716,780], footer row (menu / undo / colour-blind toggle) y∈[790,836]. A 10px dead gutter separates board from each strip and cell hit-tests are inset to a 0.46×cell radius circle, so nothing overlaps.
INPUT — TWO-STAGE TAP, which is what makes one phone work for eight people: (1) the active player taps a legal cell; a translucent ghost disc in their colour appears and every disc that WOULD flip gets a pulsing outline in that colour. Nothing is committed, so a mis-tap costs nothing and a spectator poking the board changes nothing permanent. (2) They tap CONFIRM. There are TWO confirm buttons — top and bottom — and EITHER commits, so nobody ever has to reach across the table or stand up; tapping the ghost disc a second time also commits. Tapping a different legal cell re-targets. Only the active player's colour is armed; the board carries a 3px border glow in the active colour so it is unambiguous whose turn the board belongs to. A setting "confirm on my side only" arms just the strip the active player claimed, for groups that want it stricter.
SMALL-CELL AID: on 10×10 and 12×12 boards, touchmove over the board draws a 96px circular loupe offset 80px toward the active player's side (so their finger never covers it), clipped, containing a 2.4× redraw of the board around the touch point, ringed in the active colour; lifting selects the cell under the crosshair.
TURN HANDOFF: a 260ms colour wipe sweeps both strips and the banner reads e.g. "AMBER TO PLAY". An optional "Hand It Over" mode (default off for 2P, on for 5+) shows a 1.2s full-bleed card in the next player's colour reading PASS TO ⟨NAME⟩ with an arrow pointing at their side.
PASS CLARITY (2P): a forced pass is the #1 confusion in digital Othello, so show an explicit 1.1s card — "WHITE HAS NO LEGAL MOVE — PASSES" — with the board dimmed 30% and the reason spelled out, before control returns to Black.
UNDO: tapping the last-move ring offers a single-move undo, available only until the next player commits.
NO in-game leaderboard screen; the end card shows only this table's final counts and hands the score to the platform.

## Drawing it with no assets

Zero assets. One full-viewport canvas positioned at 0,0 so clientX/clientY map directly to canvas coords — no getBoundingClientRect needed. DPR scale via ctx.setTransform(dpr,0,0,dpr,0,0). Bake static layers into `new OffscreenCanvas(w,h)` (NOT document.createElement); if OffscreenCanvas is missing, just redraw — the static board is only a few hundred ops.
1. GROUND: fill #14181A, then a radial gradient from centre (rgba(255,255,255,0.04)→transparent) and a corner vignette (transparent→rgba(0,0,0,0.55)). Optional wood: 60 horizontal strokes at alpha 0.03–0.06 in #6B5136/#2A1E12, y-warped by a hash-based value-noise function.
2. BEZEL: roundRect(radius 18) filled with a 45° linearGradient #3A4247→#0D1013; inset roundRect flat #14171A; 3px rgba(255,255,255,0.22) stroke clipped to top+left, rgba(0,0,0,0.6) clipped to bottom+right; two brass plaques as 4×14 roundRects with a #C9A227→#8A6D14 gradient at the left/right mid-edges; engraved OTHELLO wordmark drawn twice (light at y+1, dark at y).
3. FELT: roundRect(radius 6) filled with a radial gradient centred at 42%/34% of the field, #35855F(0)→#276F52(0.55)→#163F30(1.0). Procedural felt, baked ONCE: (a) 3000 dots — x=hash(i), y=hash(i+9e3), fillRect(x,y,1.5,1.5) alternating rgba(255,255,255,0.035) and rgba(0,0,0,0.05); (b) 900 fibre strokes 2–4px long at random angles, lineWidth 0.7, alpha 0.04. Never per-frame.
4. GRID: 9+9 lines strokeStyle #10382A lineWidth 1, snapped to half-device-pixels for crispness; second pass at rgba(255,255,255,0.05) offset +1px x and y for an incised-groove highlight. STAR DOTS: filled circles r = cell·0.09 at the four intersections two cells in from each corner, fill #0C2C20 plus a top-arc highlight at rgba(255,255,255,0.08). (On 10×10/12×12 keep four dots at the same 2-in offset.)
5. DISC — draw order per disc at (cx,cy,r,θ,colour): (a) contact shadow: radial-gradient ellipse centred (cx+0.10r, cy+0.16r), radii (1.02r, 0.55r), rgba(0,0,0,0.42)→transparent — the gradient IS the blur, since ctx.filter is banned; (b) side wall: ellipse rx=r, ry=r·|cos θ| offset down by wall = 0.13r·(1−|cos θ|·0.35), filled with a horizontal linearGradient #A9986E→#E4D7B0→#8E7F58; (c) top face: ellipse rx=r, ry=r·|cos θ|, filled with a radial gradient offset to (cx−0.30r, cy−0.34r·|cos θ|) — WHITE: #FFFDF6→#F1EDE0@0.55→#C8C0AC; ONYX: #4A4B46→#22231F@0.45→#0D0E0C; any player colour C: lighten(C,45%)→C→darken(C,42%); (d) rim stroke lineWidth 1, rgba(0,0,0,0.35) on light faces / rgba(255,255,255,0.10) on dark; (e) specular: radial-gradient ellipse at (cx−0.34r, cy−0.40r·|cos θ|), rx=0.40r, ry=0.20r·|cos θ|, rgba(255,255,255,0.28) light / 0.13 dark, fading to transparent so it has no hard edge. Cache one gradient object per (colour, radius) — regenerating gradients per disc per frame is the main perf trap.
6. COLOUR-BLIND ASSIST (toggle): hand-authored inline-SVG-style Path2D glyphs at 0.34r in the contrasting ink, one per player index — ring, dot, triangle, square, diamond, four-point star, plus, bar.
7. HUD: roundRect panels #14181A with a 1px rgba(255,255,255,0.07) top highlight; chips are pills containing the SAME disc routine at r=11; active chip glow = three concentric strokes at alpha 0.18/0.10/0.05 (no blur); territory bar is a 10px roundRect split into proportional segments, each filled with that player's gradient, remainder #0F2A21, widths tweened.
8. ROTATION: top strip renders through save/translate/rotate(π)/translate/restore around the identical draw function.
9. LOUPE: ctx.save(); clip a 96px circle; translate/scale 2.4 about the touch point; re-run the board draw; restore; stroke the circle 3px in the active colour.
10. Animation state is a flat array of {disc, startMs, durMs, fromColour, toColour}; render is a pure function of performance.now(). Total code well under 2MB in a single JS file.

## Risks

- 12x12 cells land at ~31.8px, below the 44px touch minimum — mitigated by the two-stage tap+confirm (mis-taps are free) and the 2.4x follow-finger loupe, but consider capping at 10x10 for all of 5-8P if playtests show mis-selection.
- The Rolit fallback placement (place anywhere adjacent when you cannot capture) can make 6-8P games feel swingy and random since a single late move can flip 15 third-party discs; scaling the board with player count helps, and the fallback must be gated to fire ONLY when no capture exists anywhere.
- 180-degree rotated HUD text is genuinely hard to read below ~12px — keep the top strip's minimum type at 12px Space Grotesk / 22px Bebas and never expect a far player to read the near strip.
- A 20+ disc cascade re-rendering per-disc radial gradients will drop frames on low-end phones; gradients must be cached per (colour, radius) and the felt/grid/bezel layer baked once to an OffscreenCanvas.
- Perfect information plus a flat phone means there is no natural pass-the-phone beat, so 6-8 player games can degrade into two people hunched over the screen while everyone else spectates; the dual confirm buttons, the persistent last-move ring, and the 260ms colour handoff wipe are the countermeasures.
- The mandatory-pass rule in strict 2P reads as a bug to newcomers — the explicit 1.1s 'HAS NO LEGAL MOVE - PASSES' card with the board dimmed is required, not optional.
