# badland — design brief

*Compiled from reference images the researcher downloaded and viewed.*

## Art direction

Pure-black silhouette theatre against a blown-out backlit gradient: every foreground object — creature, saw blade, tree, gear, rim — is filled #000000 with zero interior detail, and all information comes from the shape's outline read against a glowing sky. In the shots I viewed the background is a huge soft radial sun-bloom (near-white core #FDFEE7 fading through hot gold #FCFC3C into burnt orange #CB4F06 and then into near-black #240C0C at the frame edges), with 2-3 separate bloom centres per scene and layered translucent fog bands that let distant silhouettes fade to a smoky mid-tone instead of pure black. The frame is always clamped by an irregular organic black band across the top and bottom — a cave mouth with a wavy, lumpy, hand-torn edge, never a straight letterbox — plus a heavy corner vignette. Four distinct "hours" recur: DAWN amber/gold, NOON acid lime-on-emerald with white-hot shafts, DUSK orange-red bleeding into plum/rose, NIGHT cobalt-and-ice-blue with cyan star specks and a magenta laser as the only warm accent. The single point of colour in the foreground is the creature's EYES — flat saturated ovals with a white pupil dot and a soft coloured halo — which is exactly how the game identifies up to four simultaneous players. Everything is soft, organic, and slightly wet-looking; nothing is geometric except the machinery (saw blades, gears, rails), which is deliberately hard-edged and menacing against the organic mush.

## Palette

## Typography

Bebas Neue is the primary and near-exact match. In the menu screenshots I read ("SINGLE PLAYER", "COOP", "VERSUS", "FROGMIND'S CHOICE", "744 / CLONES", "MOST PLAYED"), all display type is heavy CONDENSED ALL-CAPS white sans with tight tracking, short descenders and a chunky uniform stroke — that is Bebas Neue's exact register. Use it for every HUD number, tab label, countdown numeral, round banner and the game title. Nunito Sans at 700/800 is the secondary: BADLAND's mixed-case level titles ("Machine of doom", "Magic touch") are a rounded humanist bold, and Nunito Sans's soft terminals match the organic, slightly cartoonish feel far better than Inter's neutral grotesque. Rule: Bebas Neue for anything uppercase or numeric, Nunito Sans 800 for the few mixed-case player names and body strings, nothing else. Explicitly avoid Press Start 2P (wrong era — BADLAND is painterly, not pixel), Space Mono (too technical), and all three serifs (Cormorant/DM Serif would read as a fairy tale, not a bio-mechanical forest). Canvas has no reliable letterSpacing, so draw Bebas headings glyph-by-glyph with a manual +2px advance to reproduce the game's airy tracking on tabs.

## Shape language

Two opposed vocabularies. ORGANIC: everything natural is built from clusters of overlapping circles — in the dusk screenshot the foreground rock and foliage is literally a chain of 30-60 tangent black discs of varying radius, which is why the outline reads as lumpy and bulbous rather than polygonal. Trunks and vines are tapered strokes (round caps, width falling off along a quadratic). The creature "Clony" is a fuzzy black ball with a jagged fur fringe (roughly 26-30 short triangular spikes alternating between r and 1.18r around the rim), 4-5 tapering tentacle legs hanging below that trail and lag behind motion, and two small horn tufts on top; big grown versions keep the identical silhouette scaled up 6x. MECHANICAL: circular saw blades with ~20 raked triangular teeth and 3 concentric faint inner arc cutouts, gears with rectangular teeth and spoked hubs, straight rails, hinged plank platforms bolted at pivot dots, thin needle spikes. Power-up pods are a hybrid: a small black spiked ball (12-14 teeth) with a glowing radial core in the effect's colour. Nothing has an outline stroke, nothing has a highlight — pure fills only. Silhouettes never overlap ambiguously: there is always a bloom placed behind a hazard so its shape reads.

## UI chrome

Chrome is black lozenges floating on the live game background — no panels, no cards, no rectangles with borders. Every button/label is a heavily rounded black slab (fill #060606, corner radius ~18-22px) whose corners are slightly irregular/hand-wobbled rather than perfect, with white Bebas caps sitting directly on it and generous horizontal padding. Selected state is a single bright lime-green outline ring (~#6CA903 to #8CE818, 4px) hugging the lozenge — I saw exactly this on the active "SINGLE PLAYER" tab, and it is the only UI colour in the entire interface. Counters use a stacked lockup: a very large number over a small caps label beneath it ("744" over "CLONES"), both white, both inside the same black lozenge, top-right corner. Level thumbnails are drawn as slightly barrel-curved CRT-screen shapes (rounded square, edges bowed outward) with a thick black bezel, stacked like a deck with 2-3 coloured slivers peeking out on the right. Badges are diagonal corner ribbons (blue with white caps). Icons are pure white line/solid glyphs (a magnifier, a globe). No drop shadows, no gradients on UI, no translucency — flat black on painterly background, maximum contrast.

## Motion

Weighty, floaty, physics-first — nothing is tweened, everything is simulated. The creature falls under strong gravity (~2.1 arena-radii/s^2) and holding the input produces repeated discrete FLAPS at ~9Hz (each an impulse plus a wing-scale pop), never a smooth jetpack thrust, so the ascent visibly stair-steps. Air drag multiplies velocity by ~0.992/frame giving a terminal speed and a heavy, laggy feel. Bodies are round rigid bodies with restitution ~0.55 that shove each other convincingly — pushing a rival into a blade is a first-class verb. Squash-and-stretch along the velocity vector (up to +28% at high speed) and 3-frame trailing lag on the tentacle legs sell the softness. Death is silhouette confetti: the body pops into ~14 small black fur shards flung outward, one white flash disc fading over 180ms, 90ms of 6px screen shake — never red, never gore. The background breathes: bloom radii oscillate ±4% at 0.25Hz and fog bands drift laterally, so a static camera still feels alive. Particulate spore motes drift constantly with sine bob at 0.35 alpha; night scenes add ~120 twinkling cyan specks. Hazards telegraph: a laser draws a 700ms thin white aiming line before the beam. UI motion is minimal — countdown numerals scale 1.6→1.0 on easeOutBack, lozenges pop in at 120ms, nothing slides.

## Mechanics

ORIGINAL BADLAND, EXACT RULES. (1) INPUT: exactly one binary verb. Press = the creature flaps its wings, gaining upward and slight forward momentum; hold = repeated flaps; release = free fall. There is NO left/right control and no other action, ever. (2) AUTO-SCROLL: the camera advances rightward at a level-authored speed and cannot be stopped. The LEFT screen edge is lethal — any clone that falls behind is destroyed; you can only move left via world forces (fans, explosions, being shoved). Being crushed between scenery and the advancing edge is a core death. (3) CLONES: the "Clony" reproduces via clone pods that spawn +1, +3 or +5 copies. Every clone receives the IDENTICAL input in the same frame — one finger drives the entire swarm. Clones are physical bodies that collide with the world and with each other, so a swarm behaves like a sack of balls and is used as ballast to hold levers/buttons down. (4) POWER-UP PROPAGATION RULE (the signature rule): if ANY single clone absorbs a pod, the effect applies to the ENTIRE swarm instantly. Pods are magnetically attracted to a nearby clone before being absorbed. (5) WIN CONDITION: exactly ONE surviving clone must reach the level exit. Extra survivors do not gate completion; they raise your score/star rating and mission progress. Losing every clone = restart. (6) CHECKPOINTS: single-player levels have checkpoints; multiplayer has none — a wipe restarts the round. (7) POWER-UPS (fan names — Frogmind never published official names, flag this): GROW (radius and mass up), SHRINK (radius and mass down, required to fit gaps), SPEED UP / SLOW DOWN (horizontal velocity multiplier), TIME SLOW / TIME FAST (global timescale), CLONE MULTIPLIER (+N bodies), STICKY (adheres to surfaces, restitution 0), BOUNCY (high restitution), ROLL/SPIN (locks constant angular velocity CW or CCW, turning clones into wheels), and direction/gravity flip. Effects are timed; a new pod on the same axis REPLACES the old one rather than stacking. (8) HAZARDS: rotating circular saw blades, gear trains, crushers and pistons, needle spikes, hinged plank platforms on pivots, wind fans, sticky cockleburs, explosive pods, rotating laser emitters, rising liquid, and mazes with lethal walls. (9) STRUCTURE: two "Days" (Day I = 40 levels) split across four palettes/stages — DAWN, NOON, DUSK, NIGHT — plus a user level editor ("Level World"). (10) MULTIPLAYER, up to 4 humans on ONE device: each player owns a screen region to tap and is identified solely by EYE COLOUR (I confirmed cyan, magenta, yellow and orange eyes in the shots). COOP: shared level, power-ups benefit everyone, the team fails if anyone is wiped out. VERSUS: purpose-built arenas, "survival of the fittest with no rules" — you may deliberately shove rivals into saw blades; last creature alive wins the round.

## One-phone adaptation

"BADLAND: THE HOLLOW" — 2-8 players, portrait phone FLAT ON THE TABLE, never picked up. HIDDEN INFORMATION: there is none. BADLAND is a pure-reflex perfect-information physics game, so there is NO pass-the-phone and NO privacy screen anywhere in this design — the phone stays flat for the whole session and the only design problem is orientation and finger real estate. SOLVING ORIENTATION: do not port the horizontal side-scroller. Replace it with a circular cave chamber centred on the screen where GRAVITY POINTS RADIALLY OUTWARD toward the rim. "Down" is now "toward the wall", which is identical from every seat, so the play field has no up and reads correctly for a player at any edge. The scroll pressure of the original becomes a constant clockwise tangential CURRENT (0.30R px/s^2) that drags every swarm around the ring, and the lethal left edge becomes the rim, which is lined with rotating saw teeth. Holding your pad flaps your whole swarm INWARD toward the centre at 9Hz. INPUT ZONES: N non-overlapping rounded-black pads hug the screen perimeter outside the arena circle, each rotated so its label faces its owner (bottom pads 0deg, top pads 180deg, left 90deg, right -90deg via ctx.rotate). Allocation on a 390x844 portrait screen: 2P = full-width bottom bar + full-width top bar (160px tall each); 3P = full bottom bar + two top half-bars; 4P = two bottom + two top quadrant bars; 5P = 2 bottom, 2 top, 1 right side bar; 6P = 2 bottom, 2 top, left + right side bars; 7P = 3 bottom, 2 top, left + right; 8P = 3 bottom, 3 top, left + right. Every pad is >=88x88 CSS px with a 6px dead gutter between neighbours so nobody steals a neighbour's tap, and the arena circle is inset at least padDepth+12px so no finger ever occludes play. IDENTITY: each player is a pure-black Clony distinguished only by EYE COLOUR plus a matching glow halo — never tint the body, that would destroy the silhouette rule. Accents lifted from the real game: P1 cyan #18A0E0, P2 magenta #D808B8, P3 yellow #E8E818, P4 orange #FF7A18, then P5 lime #7CE81E, P6 violet #9B5CFF, P7 rose #FF3C6E, P8 ice #6EE8D8; the pad rim glows the same colour so a player can always find their seat. SEAT CLAIM: a 5-second window where holding a pad hatches your Clony at that pad's arena angle — self-serve, no lobby, no keyboard. ROUND RULES: any body touching a rim tooth, a free saw blade, a laser beam, or leaving the arena is destroyed; you are eliminated when your LAST body dies. CLONE pods grant +2 bodies driven by your one pad (the signature mechanic survives intact) and the BADLAND propagation rule holds — a pod absorbed by any one of your bodies affects your whole swarm. Pods: GROW (gold core, r x1.6 mass x2.6), SHRINK (cyan, r x0.6), CLONE (white, instant +2), SPIN (violet, +/-6 rad/s), STICKY (green, restitution 0 and a 1.2s cling-instead-of-die grace on the rim), BOUNCY (magenta, restitution 0.95); 8s timers, same-axis pods replace rather than stack. Bodies collide elastically at restitution 0.55, so shoving a rival into the teeth is the intended kill. ANTI-STALL: the chamber constricts, R shrinking 100%->55% over 50s on easeInOutCubic, then a crusher ring advances 6px/s — no timeouts, no draws. Last swarm alive takes the round; best-of-5, simultaneous deaths both score. COOP mode (2-8): no elimination, one shared body pool, waves ramp every 20s, power-ups benefit everyone per the original coop rule, score = seconds survived. BETWEEN ROUNDS: the scoreboard is a ring of pips around the arena centre, one at each player's seat angle and rotated to face outward, so all 8 people read their own win count right-side-up without anyone touching the phone.

## Drawing it with no assets

ZERO ASSETS — everything is canvas 2D fills, gradients and math. BACKGROUND (the whole look): 2-3 sun blooms per palette, each a createRadialGradient(x,y,0,x,y,R) with stops [0 rgba(253,254,231,0.95), 0.18 rgba(252,252,60,0.55), 0.45 rgba(203,79,6,0.28), 1 transparent] painted with globalCompositeOperation='lighter' over a base createLinearGradient running the palette from hot centre to #240C0C at the frame. This alone reproduces BADLAND's glow — the canvas filter/blur property is banned and NOT needed, because stacked low-alpha radial gradients ARE the blur. Add 3 fog bands as wide ellipses filled with a vertical gradient of the palette mid-tone at alpha 0.10-0.18, also 'lighter', drifting laterally on sin(t). Cache this entire static background ONCE per palette into an OffscreenCanvas (document.createElement is banned, so OffscreenCanvas is the only offscreen route; if unavailable, render the background at 0.5 scale each frame and upscale with drawImage). CAVE FRAME: build the top and bottom black bands as a path where y = base + 26*sin(i*0.7+s1) + 14*sin(i*1.9+s2) + 7*sin(i*4.3+s3) across ~90 samples with fixed seeds, closed out to the screen corners and filled #000 — deterministic organic wobble, no noise library. ORGANIC SILHOUETTES: never draw a polygon. Walk a quadratic spline and stamp 30-60 overlapping ctx.arc discs of radius 8-40px along it, all filled #000 in one path — that is literally how the game's foliage and rock read. Trunks/vines = 12 tapered stroke segments with round caps and linearly decreasing lineWidth. CLONY: ctx.beginPath, loop a=0..2pi in 28 steps alternating lineTo(r) and lineTo(r*1.18) for the fur fringe, close, fill #000; then 5 tentacle legs as filled quadratic teardrops hanging opposite the thrust vector with 3-frame positional lag; 2 horn tufts as small triangles; eyes = two ellipses filled with the player accent plus a white 2px pupil dot offset toward velocity, each backed by a radial-gradient halo of the accent at alpha 0.5 radius 3.5x the eye ('lighter') so the eye still reads at 8px. Squash: ctx.transform scale along the velocity axis by 1+clamp(|v|/900,0,0.28). SAW BLADE: circle path with 20 teeth, each tooth a triangle raked ~12deg in the spin direction (alternate r and r*1.28), filled #000, plus 3 concentric arcs stroked 3px in #141414 for the faint inner cutouts I saw. GEAR: circle + 16 rectangular teeth + hub circle + 5 spoke strokes. LASER: three stacked strokes on the same line — 18px rgba(accent,0.14) 'lighter', 6px rgba(accent,0.6), 2px #FFFFF4 — plus a radial bloom disc at the emitter. POWER-UP POD: 12-tooth spiked black ball with a radial-gradient core in the effect colour. PARTICLES: 40 spore motes (small filled circles, alpha 0.35, sine bob) and 120 star specks in the night palette. TEXTURE: a fixed table of 900 pseudo-random points drawn at alpha 0.03 warm white, jittered by a per-frame offset — grain with no image. VIGNETTE: full-screen radial gradient transparent->#000 at alpha 0.85. UI: a roundRectWobble(x,y,w,h,r,seed) helper drawing 4 quadratic corners with seeded jitter of +/-2px to reproduce the hand-drawn lozenges, filled #060606; active state strokes it 4px in #8CE818 with a 12px 'lighter' glow ring. SVG PATH DATA is optional and only worth it for the two icons (magnifier, globe) via Path2D — everything else is cheaper as primitives. Total code well under 2MB and no remote fetch of any kind (Google Fonts link only for Bebas Neue + Nunito Sans).

## Risks

- Simultaneous touch cap: 8 concurrent touches exceeds many Android digitizers (commonly 5, sometimes 10). Mitigate by switching to impulse-tap mode (one tap = 260ms of thrust, no holding) at 7-8 players and warning at seat-claim if fewer than N touches ever register together.
- Pad size collapse at 8 players: three pads across a 390px portrait screen leaves ~120px each. Enforce a hard >=88x88 CSS px minimum with 6px gutters, and refuse to offer 7-8 player seats on screens narrower than 360px.
- Radial gravity is unfamiliar — players instinctively expect down = screen bottom and will die repeatedly in the first round. Mitigate with a permanent darkening gradient toward the rim, faint animated 'fall lines' pointing outward, and a 6-second no-death warmup on round 1.
- Fill-rate: stacked low-alpha radial gradients plus 'lighter' compositing is expensive on low-end phones. The static background MUST be cached to an OffscreenCanvas (document.createElement is banned, so there is no fallback canvas element) or rendered at half resolution and upscaled.
- Pure black on a dark palette can vanish on a dim screen in a bright room. Every hazard needs a 2px #141414 edge lift and a guaranteed bloom placed behind it, and the NIGHT palette must never be used for the first round.
- Finger occlusion: with 8 hands on the phone, fingers will cross the play field. Inset the arena circle by padDepth+12px and never place a hazard or pod inside the outer 10% of the screen.
