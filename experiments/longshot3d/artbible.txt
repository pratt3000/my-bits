LONGSHOT — ART BIBLE (applies to every asset in this project)

SETTING
An East African high-veld game reserve at first light. Dry season.

STYLE
Stylised realism. Simplified planes and clear edges, not photoreal and not
cartoon. Every asset must read at 400 metres through a rifle scope, so the
silhouette carries the identity — fine filigree that disappears past 50 m is
wasted geometry.

PALETTE
Sun-bleached ochre, dry-grass gold, olive-grey scrub, warm bone white, deep
umber shadow. Cool teal appears only in sky and shadow. No saturated primaries.

MATERIALS
Matte and dusty throughout. No chrome, no gloss, no emissive, no glass unless
the object is glass. A fine grit or dust pass over everything, heavier in
crevices. Metal is blued or parkerised, never polished.

SCALE
Honest metric proportion. An adult cheetah is 1.2 m at the shoulder; a rifle is
1.2 m long.

TRIANGLE BUDGET
Environment props under 4,000 triangles. Animals under 5,000. The player's
weapon, which is on screen constantly, may go to 12,000.

CONSTRUCTION
Repeated surface features (scales, spots, tread, rivets, ribs) must be cut into
the parent surface as ONE continuous mesh, never as separate blocks.
Anything that has to move in the engine — a leg, a bolt, a magazine, a scope
turret — must be its own named part.
